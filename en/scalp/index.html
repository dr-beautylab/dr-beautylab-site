FAQ

FAQ

Does the director do the treatment herself?
Yes. The director, a Ph.D. in Beauty Art and university professor, does every step from consultation to finish. We see only 3 clients a day, so the artist never changes halfway.
Can I visit just for a consultation first?
In-person consultations are held on the day of your treatment, before we begin, for clients who have booked. We don't take visits for consultation only. Instead, send us a photo and we'll advise you in detail via Instagram DM, LINE or phone first, feel free to ask anything.
What is part line / crown coverage?
Tiny dots are placed on the scalp to fill in areas that look thin. Pricing is fixed per area: KRW 250,000 per session for one area (business-card size). We measure with a real business card at your consultation and tell you the price on the spot.
How many sessions do I need?
Because micro-dots are layered over a pale scalp, we recommend at least 2 sessions. One session already fills the area, but density and color settle in the second. 2 sessions prepaid: KRW 450,000; 3 sessions: KRW 650,000.
I'm worried it will look obvious.
We check the density and direction of your existing hair and design before starting. The key is controlling the pigment and depth so no reddish tone remains as it fades, and this is what we focus on most.
Do you treat men?
Yes. Many of our brow, crown and hairline clients are men. Men usually can't cover it up with makeup, so if a tattoo looks obvious, it shows as it is and makes your look feel off. That's exactly why we match the density and direction of your natural hair, so it looks like your own and nothing looks done.
How long does it last?
Deeper pigment lasts longer, but blurring and color shifts also stay longer. We work at a depth that fades naturally over time. Longevity varies a lot with scalp and hair condition and lifestyle, so we explain it in person at your consultation.
