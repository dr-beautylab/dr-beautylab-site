What is scalp micropigmentation (SMP)?

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) is a semi-permanent pigmentation technique applied to the scalp to create the visual appearance of fuller hair density. At Dr. Beauty Lab, the goal is to fill in sparse areas while keeping the result looking as natural as the client's own existing hair.

What areas can scalp micropigmentation (SMP) cover?

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) at Dr. Beauty Lab is offered for three areas of the scalp.

  • Hairline
  • Crown
  • Parting line

How is scalp micropigmentation (SMP) different from scalp cover?

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) and scalp cover are not different procedures at Dr. Beauty Lab; "scalp cover" is simply another name used for the same scalp micropigmentation (SMP) service. Clients do not need to choose between the two, since they refer to one and the same treatment.

How does Dr. Beauty Lab do it?

Dr. Beauty Lab applies pigment in a way that each individual mark is not visible to the naked eye and can only be seen under 1000x magnification, so the result reads as filled-in hair density rather than visible dots. Before designing the pattern, Director Park Hyunjung reviews the density and texture of the client's existing hair. Pigment type and depth of application are controlled with the aim of preventing a reddish tone as the color settles over time. Only US- and EU-certified materials are used, and single-use items are opened in front of the client. Director Park Hyunjung handles consultation and the full procedure herself, without a change of practitioner partway through.
