How is eyebrow semi-permanent makeup different from scalp micropigmentation (SMP)?

Eyebrow semi-permanent makeup and scalp micropigmentation (SMP) are both pigment-based procedures, but they are applied to different areas and serve different purposes. Eyebrow semi-permanent makeup shapes and fills the eyebrows using hair stroke, powder (shading), or combination techniques. Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) is applied to the hairline, parting line, or crown area of the scalp to create the appearance of denser hair. At Dr. Beauty Lab, scalp micropigmentation (SMP) and what the studio calls "scalp cover" refer to the same procedure, not two separate treatments.

How does Dr. Beauty Lab design eyebrow semi-permanent makeup?

Dr. Beauty Lab designs each eyebrow based on facial shape, the proportion of facial features, and skin tone before choosing a brow style. The studio states that the goal is a natural result that looks like the client's own eyebrows rather than an obviously altered look. Pigment and application depth are adjusted with the aim of avoiding a reddish or bluish residual tone as the pigment fades over time. The studio uses materials certified in the United States and Europe, and disposable items are opened in front of the client.
