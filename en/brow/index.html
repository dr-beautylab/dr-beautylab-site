FAQ

FAQ

Does the director do the treatment herself?
Yes. The director, a Ph.D. in Beauty Art and university professor, does every step from consultation to finish. We see only 3 clients a day, so you're never handed off to another artist.
Can I visit just for a consultation first?
In-person consultations are held on the day of your treatment, before we begin, for clients who have booked. We don't take visits for consultation only. Instead, send us a photo and we'll advise you in detail via Instagram DM, LINE or phone. Feel free to ask anything.
I'm worried my brows will look unnatural or turn red.
We check your face shape and the balance of your features and confirm the design before starting. The key is controlling the pigment and depth so no reddish tone remains as the color fades, and this is what we focus on most.
Will they look too dark right after?
Right after the treatment they actually look natural. They then darken from that evening through about day 3 as the skin heals. This is completely normal. Within about 7 days the flaking finishes and they soften into a natural tone.
When should I come for the touch-up?
For all three styles, one touch-up is free if you visit within 4–8 weeks. It isn't mandatory. It fills in any areas that didn't take fully, so if the color healed well, you can skip it. After 8 weeks, a separate fee applies.
I'm only in Seoul for a short trip. Is one visit enough?
Yes. One session is enough for the color to settle beautifully on its own. If you're back in Korea within 4–8 weeks, the touch-up is free as always, but you don't have to come back for it.
How long do they last?
Deeper pigment lasts longer, but blocky shapes and reddish discoloration also stay longer. We work at a depth that fades naturally over time. Longevity varies a lot with skin type and lifestyle, so we explain it in person at your consultation.
Do you treat men's brows?
Yes. Many of our brow, crown and hairline clients are men. Most men don't wear makeup to hide it, so a tattoo that looks obvious really stands out. That's why we match the density and direction of your natural hair, so the result looks like your own.
